# The Battery Kitchen, 90 recipes

The 40 that already ship are kept exactly as they are. 50 are new: all 30 lunches,
plus 10 breakfasts and 10 dinners.

**SHIPPING** means the recipe exists today and its text is unchanged. Its lineage is a
*technique match*: the recipe already uses the technique the research credits to that
chef. It is not a claim that it was inspired by anyone, because it was written before
the research existed. **NEW** means inspired by the dish named, and it needs writing.

Tiers: ALL is pro, advanced, intermediate and beginner. IB is intermediate and beginner only.
Tags mark the quota recipes: fish, organ, fire, starch, fruit.
