## 1. The model

Health depends on cells building, holding, spending and coordinating electrical gradients across membranes. There are two batteries: the inner mitochondrial membrane and the plasma membrane, and they are coupled, because the second is maintained by pumps the first pays for.

Five physical currencies move through the system: photons, electrons, protons and water, electric fields, and membrane structure. Oxygen is the terminal electron acceptor, and the roughly 1.1 volt span from NADH to water is the entire energy budget of aerobic life.

**The operative definition of health:**

> Health emerges when environmental timing, photon exposure, nutrient-derived reducing equivalents, membrane architecture, proton and water dynamics, mitochondrial membrane potential and redox signalling stay appropriately coordinated in space and in time. Disease, in this model, is not primarily a shortage of energy. It is a loss of coordination between the parts of a system that must agree in order to work.

Health is tissue-appropriate voltage and the ability to change it on demand, not maximum voltage. Coherence, not maximum.

### Rules

1. Frontier dimensions carry no score, no placeholder, no null-valued bar, no coverage penalty. They are model structure, not missing patient data.
2. No surface computes or displays a coverage figure, a fraction, or an "n of 8" string. The composite runs over the scored dimensions and is complete by definition.
3. Score headers read "5 measured dimensions".
4. A participant's score is never presented as incomplete because the scientific model extends past current instrumentation.
5. `evidence_basis` renders on each scored tile as a quiet label. Subtle is acceptable. Absent is not.

### Frontier copy, verbatim

Section intro:

> Fundamental to the Human Battery model. Not included in your score until they can be measured with sufficient clinical validity.

**Charge:**

> Mitochondrial membrane potential (ΔΨm). Direct whole-person clinical measurement is not yet routinely available.

**Redox:**

> Mitochondrial redox state. Current biomarkers reveal pieces of the system, but do not provide a direct whole-person mitochondrial redox measurement.

**Leak:**

## 3. Corrected positions

### 3.1 Light

**Superseded:** light is biological timing information, never energy.

**Canonical:** light is information, and light is a minor energy input.

- Job one is timing, through the melanopsin cells in the retina. This is the larger effect.
- Job two is a small amount of real photochemistry in the skin through chromophores and opsins: UVB makes vitamin D, UVA releases nitric oxide.
- Skin absorbs a great deal of solar energy, but almost all of it becomes heat, because the body has no machinery to convert photons into ATP. Absorbing radiant energy and converting it into biological work are different things.

**Still forbidden:** that sunlight charges the body the way a charger fills a phone.

Citations: Berson, Dunn & Takao, *Science*, 2002 (timing). Holick, MacLaughlin, Clark et al., *Science*, 1980, 210:203 (vitamin D photosynthesis). Liu et al., *Journal of Investigative Dermatology*, 2014 (nitric oxide).

Headings that state the superseded position as a headline, such as "Light is timing, not fuel", are replaced rather than softened.

### 3.2 Seasonal eating

**Superseded:** summer fruit carries higher energy electrons, so out-of-season food sends a mismatched seasonal signal.

**Canonical, and the sentence to use verbatim:**

> Electrons are indistinguishable. Electron environments are not.

Electrons carry no information about where or when food grew. The molecular systems that carry them do, and the growing environment is what builds those systems. Food does not need to write a latitude onto an electron to carry environmental information.

#### 3.2.1 The thermodynamic limit, which every surface must respect

ΔG°′ = −nFΔE°′. NAD⁺/NADH sits at about −0.32 V, O₂/H₂O at about +0.82 V, a span of 1.14 V, roughly 220 kJ/mol for the two electrons NADH carries.

**Those potentials are properties of the carriers, not of the meal.** Macronutrients converge on common intermediates and leave as NADH and FADH₂ whatever was eaten. A polyphenol oxidising at +0.25 V on a carbon electrode has no thermodynamic route into the proton-motive force. The standard potential is fixed; only the *ratio* of NADH to NAD⁺ moves with a meal, by tens of millivolts, not volts.

Therefore **no surface, coach output or marketing copy may state or imply that a food's antioxidant content, ORAC value, redox potential or electrochemical measurement is an energy input to mitochondria.** The USDA withdrew its own ORAC database in May 2012 for exactly this reason.

#### 3.2.2 What is established

| Statement | Tier |
| --- | --- |
| Growing conditions change the redox-active chemistry of plants, in chamber studies with genetics, soil and nutrition held constant | established |
| The direction is compound specific, cultivar specific and often opposite within one leaf. Cold raises spinach ascorbate ~41%; drought raises anthocyanins while lowering vitamin C; carotenoid optima sit at 30 °C in kale and 10 °C in spinach | established |
| Ten days of ordinary refrigerated transport plus shelf time removes 60 to 80% of broccoli phenolics and glucosinolates, larger than any seasonal contrast in the literature | established |
| Glucose oxidation yields ~5 NADH per FADH₂, fat oxidation ~2, changing CoQ pool reduction and reverse electron transport at complex I | established |
| Electrochemical fingerprints discriminate species, cultivar, season and geographic origin, but **not** farming method | emerging |
| The rise in plasma antioxidant capacity after fruit is urate from fructose metabolism, not the flavonoids | strong |

**There is no single "more antioxidant in summer" dial.** Any copy implying one is wrong on the plant science, before it is wrong on the physiology.

#### 3.2.3 The hypothesis, and its amendment

Named in the book: **the Seasonal Redox-Mismatch Hypothesis**. Photoperiod sets the organism's metabolic state; food sets substrate supply; the two were historically coupled and modern food systems uncouple them; chronic mismatch may alter mitochondrial behaviour.

**Amended in the same chapter, and the amendment is canonical.** The redox limb does not survive. The substrate limb does. The mismatch is framed around substrate composition, lipid species and microbial metabolites meeting a photoperiod-set state, never around redox capacity delivered by food.

**Tier:** hypothesis, `is_authors_model` true, with a stated falsifiable prediction and a 2 x 2 design. Never presented as established.

#### 3.2.4 The evidence against, which ships with the claim

Any surface that states the hypothesis must be able to state these too:

- The cleanest factorial test (F344 rats, photoperiod x high fat diet, interaction tested explicitly) found **independent mechanisms and no interaction** at the genes that carry the photoperiodic signal.
- The effect is strain specific within a single rodent species.
- Human photoperiodism is inconsistent; a leading review concludes some people may be photoresponsive and others are not, and a 2024 review notes human data are very rare and could run in the *opposite* direction to nocturnal rodents.
- **No human trial of in-season versus out-of-season food with metabolic outcomes exists.** Saying so is required, not optional.
- The one strong food x photoperiod interaction (mice, 2025) runs through PERIOD2 phosphorylation driven by dietary unsaturated fat, not through mitochondrial redox. It may be cited for the shape of the hypothesis and never for its mechanism.

### 3.3 Water

**Canonical:** ordered, interfacial water is part of the proton-transfer machinery inside the respiratory complexes. It is not an energy store.

Established: a 2.7 angstrom structure of complex I resolved 275 ordered water molecules forming proton-translocation pathways (Grba and Hirst, *Nature Structural and Molecular Biology*, 2020).

**Not claimed, and not refuted either.** That structured water receives the energy released by electrons, stores it, and later spends it on biochemical reactions is an open question, not a closed one. There is no evidence for it today and no surface may state it. But unevidenced and impossible are different words, and this file uses the first one on purpose.

*What makes it hard.* The energy would have to be held somewhere. The correlation work in 3.3.1 says bulk water forgets a molecular configuration in picoseconds, and a protein hydration shell only slows that by a factor of roughly 2 to 3. Storage on a biochemically useful timescale needs something orders of magnitude longer than either figure, and nothing measured so far provides it.

*What would open it.* A measurement showing that water at a confined biological site holds correlation far longer than the hydration-shell figure, plus a demonstrated route by which that correlation does work rather than merely persisting. The first half is the experiment already named in 3.3.1, so this is not an idle possibility, it is a testable one waiting on an instrument.

*Operative rule.* `evidence_tier` is `hypothesis`, `is_authors_model` false, since it is not the author's proposal either. The coach declines the claim and says why it declines it, in the language above. It never calls the idea disproven, and it never presents it as live science.

#### 3.3.1 Molecular correlation, and the words the product may use

The word "memory", applied to water, is never used on its own in any surface. The defensible quantity is the normalized time-correlation function C(t), which measures how much of a molecular configuration survives after a given interval. It falls to zero. The only question is how fast.

| Statement | Tier |
| --- | --- |
| Hydrogen bonds in liquid water break and reform in picoseconds, and the correlation decays with a power-law tail rather than a simple exponential | established (Luzar and Chandler, *Nature*, 1996, 379:55) |
| The exponent of that tail tracks the dimensionality of the space the molecules diffuse in, as t to the power of minus D over 2 | emerging (Qin et al., *Nature Communications*, 2025, 16:6555) |
| Water in a protein hydration shell is slowed by a factor of roughly 2 to 3, not orders of magnitude | established (Sterpone, Stirnemann and Laage, *JACS*, 2012, 134:4116; Laage, Elsaesser and Hynes, *Chemical Reviews*, 2017, 117:10694) |
| Confinement inside proton channels and against membranes lengthens correlation enough to have functional consequences | hypothesis, `is_authors_model` true. Named **The Biological Water Correlation Hypothesis** |

**The hypothesis, stated as the book states it:** biological water is an active component of cellular bioenergetics rather than an inert solvent. Confinement, interfaces, proteins, membranes, ions and electric fields alter its molecular dynamics, and those changes may influence hydrogen-bond connectivity, proton transport, protein dynamics and electrochemical energy conversion. The duration and spatial extent of the effect is stated as a correlation function, never as persistent "water memory".

**The falsifier, which ships with the claim:** measure the binding autocorrelation exponent for the waters in a respiratory complex proton channel. An exponent near minus one half supports the hypothesis. An exponent near minus three halves means the confined water forgets at bulk speed and the hypothesis is dead.

**The principle, split honestly.** "Water is not merely the medium surrounding the battery. At molecular interfaces, water is part of the circuit." The first sentence and the words "part of the circuit" are defensible today, because proton conduction through resolved water wires is the circuit. Any extension of that principle to bulk body water, to drinking water, or to water outside a protein is not defensible and is not made.

**Prohibited phrasings**, in copy, coach output and marketing alike: that water stores information; that water remembers substances; that drinking a particular water changes cellular structure; any use of C(t) or the exponent to describe a participant.

#### 3.3.2 Interfacial water as a frontier metric

`interfacial_water` is **not** added as a ninth state dimension and gets no tile, no score and no placeholder. It is recorded in the model as a research variable only.

What measures it today, none of it in a person: ultrafast two-dimensional infrared spectroscopy, terahertz and dielectric relaxation, NMR relaxometry and NOE, quasi-elastic and inelastic neutron scattering, molecular dynamics simulation, and hydrogen to deuterium kinetic isotope experiments.

What a clinical version would require, in order: a measurement that works in intact tissue rather than a purified sample, a signal that separates hydration-layer water from bulk water in vivo, a demonstrated link between that signal and a bioenergetic outcome, and reproducibility across labs. None of these four exists. Until the first two exist, nothing in the product reports this quantity.

## 4. The Human Battery Score

The Score is a proxy assembled from what can currently be measured, standing in for a state that cannot yet be measured directly. It is not a validated clinical instrument.

Required on every surface that displays it, visible rather than buried in a footer:

> Your Battery Score is a proxy, assembled from what can be measured today. It is not a validated clinical measure.

The Score method may never change mid-program for a participant. It may change between cohorts. Every score row records the `score_method` version that produced it.

## 5. Two evidence fields

These are different axes. Do not merge them into one column.

**`evidence_tier`** on knowledge passages, citations and claims. Answers: how good is the science behind this statement.

| Value | Consumer badge |
| --- | --- |
| established | We know this |
| strong | We are confident |
| emerging | Early evidence |
| contested | Published, and argued about |
| hypothesis | Dr. Micah's idea, being tested |
| unsupported | never shown to users; retained so the coach can decline it |

Plus the separate boolean `is_authors_model`.

**`evidence_basis`** on state dimensions. Answers: how did this participant's number get here.

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| measured | one direct test or log |
| calculated | a composite of several markers |
| frontier | no participant value; model structure |

Basis describes how the dimension's value is derived, not how many instruments touched it. One test may legitimately feed two dimensions on different bases.

**Marker roles:** every lab marker carries `marker_role` of `scored` or `referable`. A referable marker is a clinically meaningful result returned to the person and flagged when out of range, belonging to no dimension. Nothing is left unmapped.

### Coach rules

State the tier on every claim. Never upgrade a tier. Never average two. Say plainly when standing on the author's working model. Never call a score incomplete because of a frontier dimension.

## 6. Prohibited

Never build, integrate, claim or imply:

- any feature claiming to measure cellular charge or redox state in a participant
- water structuring devices, or any claim that a particular water is structured, energized or informational
- any claim that a food's antioxidant content, ORAC value, redox potential or electrochemical measurement is an energy input to mitochondria
- any presentation of seasonal eating as "summer food carries more energy", or of season as a single antioxidant dial
- magnetic or spin-based devices
- photon emission as a health readout
- serum vitamin D as a proxy for light exposure (D3 is the last step in the programme and only in winter, so serum vitamin D still measures whatever supplementation happened at least as much as sun exposure; and Chapter 7 argues the vitamin D trials underperformed precisely because they tested a molecule rather than sunlight)
- any product link in a paid deliverable without an explicit statement that no commission is taken, and an evidence tier beside it

Grounding is an optional experiment labelled hypothesis, never a prescribed requirement.
